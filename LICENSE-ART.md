## About this artwork

Bun-chan (文ちゃん) is drawn after the author's pet bearded dragon. The first draft of the pixel art was
made with an AI tool, and the author then redrew it by hand in the editor included in this repository.
How far copyright covers AI-assisted work differs between countries; this notice applies to whatever
rights the author holds in the artwork.
